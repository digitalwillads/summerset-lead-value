From ActiveCampaign and WelcomeHome, every form fill November 2025 to July 2026, followed to move-in. Prepared for the October 2 call.

A visit request is worth about 11 times a brochure download. Performance Max bought brochures that never moved in.

Performance Max: keep it off

From April to July the prior agency's Performance Max spent $22,082 and logged 582 brochure downloads at about $38 each. ActiveCampaign tagged 462 of those people "Paid Search".

Where Search stands now (August and September)

Search spent $16,033 and logged 39 brochures, 6 visit requests, 2 advisor requests and 93 calls. At the rates above, the forms alone point to about 1.5 move-ins. Calls do not reach WelcomeHome yet, so we cannot yet show what they become.

Tracking, fixed October 1

Google Ads click→Form on info.summersetsl.com→ActiveCampaign→WelcomeHome tour and move-in→Back to Google Ads
  1. The Google click ID now carries from the main site to the form pages, so ActiveCampaign records which leads came from Google Ads. Before this it was blank on every contact.
  2. Every brochure, visit and advisor form now reaches Google Ads from ActiveCampaign. September's 106 form fills were sent too.
  3. Tours and move-ins from WelcomeHome now go back to Google Ads, so we can see which keywords produce residents, not just form fills.
